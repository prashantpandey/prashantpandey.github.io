I am an Assistant Professor in the [Khoury College of Computer Sciences](https://www.khoury.northeastern.edu/people/prashant-pandey/) Northeastern University, Boston, where I direct the [SALT Systems Lab](https://saltsystemslab.github.io/) and co-direct the [Data Lab](https://db.khoury.northeastern.edu/). I am also an Affiliate Faculty in the Performance and Algorithms Research Group at [Berkeley Lab](https://crd.lbl.gov/divisions/amcr/computer-science-amcr/par/).

My goal as a researcher is to build scalable data systems with strong theoretical guarantees and employ them to democratize next-generation data analyses. My work combines **filters and compact data structures**, **I/O-efficient algorithms**, and **high-performance and GPU computing**, applied across **computational biology**, stream processing, and storage.

> **Prospective PhD students (Fall 2027):** I am recruiting **1–2 PhD students** to work on **large-scale GPU computing and AI systems**. Projects include long-context LLM inference and serving ([Iceberg Attention](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2610.iceberg-attention-long-context-decoding), [InferScale](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27090)), GPU vector search ([Jasper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.07048), VLDB '26; Directional Beam Search, VecDB '26), and adaptive filters (Aeris Filter, SIGMOD '26; To Adapt or Not to Adapt, Best of SIGMOD '26; ThimbleQF, SIGMOD '27).
>
> I am looking for students with a strong background in one or more of: **data structures and algorithms**, **GPU programming (CUDA)**, **LLM inference**, and **database systems**.
>
> If you are interested, please mention my name in your PhD application to Northeastern.

Previously, I was a Research Scientist at VMware Research for a year. Prior to that, I did postdocs at UC Berkeley/LBL and Carnegie Mellon University.

I love outdoors and occasionally like to scribble my experience in a [blog](https://hikingscribbles.wordpress.com/).
